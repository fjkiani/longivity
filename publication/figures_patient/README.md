## Questions to Ask Your Oncologist

Based on these figures, here are the most important questions a patient can ask:

1. **"Has my tumor been tested for biomarkers?"** (Figures 9, 12, 15)
2. **"Does this drug improve how long I live — or just how long before the tumor grows?"** (Figure 10)
3. **"Am I in the group this drug was designed for?"** (Figure 14)
4. **"Has my tumor been profiled with SELECT, ENLIGHT, or similar AI tools?"** (Figure 18)
5. **"What is my MMP9 level before starting bevacizumab?"** (Figures 11, 12)
